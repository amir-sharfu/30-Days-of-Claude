## Claude's Core Capabilities

> **[View diagram: Claude's 8 Capabilities →](https://excalidraw.com/#json=UaQfxOCBbQLL6VWqfZ6vB,1_X5PIhilfNfvTPJsWYu6w)**

Claude is one of the most versatile AI assistants available. Think of it as having expertise in many fields at once. Let's explore each area.

---

## 1. Writing & Editing

Claude is an exceptional writing partner. It can write, rewrite, improve, and edit nearly any type of text.

**What it can do:**
- Write emails, reports, essays, cover letters, resumes
- Proofread and correct grammar
- Improve clarity and tone
- Adapt writing for different audiences
- Summarize long documents into key points

**Try this:**
```
Rewrite this sentence to sound more professional:
"I want the raise because I've been working really hard and I think I deserve it."
```

**Claude might respond:**
```
"I am requesting a salary review based on my consistent contributions 
and the expanded scope of my responsibilities over the past year."
```

---

## 2. Research & Summarization

Claude can synthesize complex information and present it clearly.

**What it can do:**
- Explain complex topics in simple terms
- Summarize long articles or documents (paste the text in)
- Compare and contrast different ideas or options
- Answer factual questions (always verify important facts)
- Help you understand academic or technical papers

**Try this:**
```
Explain climate change in simple terms that a 12-year-old could understand. 
Use an analogy.
```

> ⚠️ **Remember:** Claude's knowledge has a cutoff date and it cannot browse the internet by default. For current news or real-time data, use other sources.

---

## 3. Coding & Technical Help

Claude is a powerful coding assistant, even for beginners.

**What it can do:**
- Write code in dozens of programming languages (Python, JavaScript, HTML, SQL, and more)
- Explain what code does in plain English
- Debug errors and explain what went wrong
- Suggest improvements to existing code
- Help you learn programming concepts

**Try this (even if you don't code):**
```
Write a simple Python program that asks for my name and says "Hello, [name]!"
Then explain each line of the code.
```

> 💡 **You don't need to be a programmer** to use Claude for technical tasks. You can describe what you want in plain English, and Claude will write the code.

---

## 4. Analysis & Critical Thinking

Claude can help you think through complex problems.

**What it can do:**
- Analyze arguments for strengths and weaknesses
- Review business plans or ideas
- Identify pros and cons of decisions
- Help structure your thinking on difficult problems
- Play "devil's advocate" to stress-test your ideas

**Try this:**
```
I'm thinking of starting a small online store selling handmade jewelry. 
What are the main risks and opportunities I should consider?
```

---

## 5. Creative Work

Claude has strong creative abilities.

**What it can do:**
- Write stories, poems, scripts, and song lyrics
- Brainstorm creative ideas on any topic
- Help with worldbuilding for fiction
- Write in different styles (like a specific author or genre)
- Generate names, slogans, taglines, or titles

**Try this:**
```
Write a short poem about rain from the perspective of a cat who hates 
getting wet. Keep it funny and under 8 lines.
```

---

## 6. Learning & Education

Claude is an exceptional tutor.

**What it can do:**
- Explain any topic at any level (beginner to expert)
- Create quizzes to test your knowledge
- Give analogies to make concepts click
- Break down complex subjects step by step
- Answer follow-up questions patiently (it never gets tired)

**Try this:**
```
I want to learn about the stock market. I'm a complete beginner. 
Can you explain it step by step, starting from the very basics?
```

---

## 7. Translation & Language

Claude handles many languages effectively.

**What it can do:**
- Translate text between dozens of languages
- Explain nuances and idioms in different languages
- Help you write in another language correctly
- Detect what language a piece of text is written in

**Try this:**
```
Translate this to French, Spanish, and German:
"Thank you for your help today. I really appreciate it."
```

---

## 8. Math & Logic

Claude can handle a wide range of mathematical tasks.

**What it can do:**
- Solve math problems step by step
- Explain mathematical concepts
- Help with algebra, statistics, geometry, and more
- Work through logic puzzles
- Check your calculations and explain errors

**Try this:**
```
If I invest $1,000 at 7% annual interest compounded yearly, 
how much will I have after 10 years? Show the calculation step by step.
```

> ⚠️ **Note:** For very complex or high-stakes calculations, always verify with a calculator or specialist.

## Claude's Limitations

Understanding what Claude *cannot* do is just as important:

| Limitation | What This Means |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Knowledge cutoff** | Claude's training data has a cutoff date — it doesn't know recent events |
| **Can hallucinate** | Claude can generate incorrect information confidently — always verify facts |
| **No real-time data** | Can't check today's stock prices, news, weather (by default) |
| **No persistent memory** | Doesn't remember past conversations — starts fresh each time |
| **Can't take actions** | Can't send emails, make calls, or interact with other apps (by default) |
| **Image limits** | Can view images if you share them, but can't generate images |

## Matching Tasks to Claude's Strengths

Use this quick reference to decide when to use Claude:

| Task | Use Claude? | Notes |
|------|------------|-------|
| Write or edit a document | ✅ Excellent | One of Claude's best uses |
| Get today's news | ❌ No | Use a news website |
| Learn a new concept | ✅ Excellent | Great patient teacher |
| Find a specific product price | ❌ No | Check the retailer's site |
| Debug code | ✅ Excellent | Works across many languages |
| Verify a medical diagnosis | ⚠️ Caution | Always see a doctor |
| Brainstorm ideas | ✅ Excellent | Great creative partner |
| Get real-time stock prices | ❌ No | Use a financial app |
| Summarize a long document | ✅ Excellent | Just paste the text in |

## Summary

🌕 Day 4 complete! Claude's capabilities are vast:

1. **Writing & Editing** — drafts, proofreading, tone adjustment
2. **Research & Summarization** — explanations, comparisons, summaries
3. **Coding** — writing, explaining, and debugging code
4. **Analysis** — reasoning through complex problems
5. **Creative Work** — stories, poems, brainstorming
6. **Education** — tutoring any topic at any level
7. **Translation** — dozens of languages
8. **Math & Logic** — step-by-step problem solving

And key limitations: knowledge cutoff, no real-time data, no persistent memory, potential for errors.

## Exercises

### Level 1 — Beginner

1. Try one prompt from each of the 8 capability areas listed above. Which one surprised you the most?
2. Find one thing Claude does very well. Find one thing it doesn't do well. Write a short comparison.
3. Ask Claude to create a short quiz (5 questions) on a topic you're studying or interested in. Take the quiz!

### Level 2 — Intermediate

4. Paste a paragraph from a news article or blog post and ask Claude to summarize it in 3 bullet points. How accurate is the summary?
5. Give Claude a logical puzzle or riddle. Does it solve it correctly? How does it explain its reasoning?
6. Ask Claude to write a piece of creative writing, then ask it to rewrite the same piece in the style of two different genres (e.g., thriller and comedy).

### Level 3 — Challenge

7. Test Claude's limits: Ask about a very recent event (from the last few months). What does Claude say? What does this tell you about using Claude for current information?
8. Choose a complex decision you're facing (real or hypothetical). Ask Claude to help you analyze it from multiple angles. Evaluate how useful its analysis is.
9. Pick a topic you know very well. Quiz Claude on it. How accurate is it? Where does it make mistakes? What does this tell you about verifying AI-generated information?
